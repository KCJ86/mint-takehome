## Starter Plan

**$39/month per location** _(was $29 in v1.0)_

Perfect for small restaurants, food trucks, and single-location businesses.

**Core Features:**

- ✅ Inventory Management: Track up to 50 ingredients
- ✅ Recipe Management: Store up to 20 salsa recipes
- ✅ Production Tracking: Track up to 100 batches per month
- ✅ Quality Control: Basic quality testing and scoring
- ✅ Basic Analytics: Simple reports and dashboards
- ✅ Mobile App: iOS and Android apps
- ✅ Team Management: Up to 5 team members
- ✅ Data Export: CSV export functionality

**Integrations:** Square POS, Toast POS, basic inventory systems, email notifications.

**Support:** Email support (24-48 hour response), live chat during business hours, knowledge base and video tutorials.

<Note>
  **Free Trial**: Start with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.
</Note>

**Advanced Features:**

- ✅ Unlimited Inventory: Track unlimited ingredients
- ✅ Unlimited Recipes: Store unlimited salsa recipes
- ✅ Unlimited Production: Track unlimited batches
- ✅ Advanced Analytics: Detailed insights and custom reports
- ✅ Automated Alerts: Smart notifications for low stock and quality issues
- ✅ Multi-Location Support: Manage up to 5 locations
- ✅ Advanced Team Management: Up to 25 team members with role-based permissions
- ✅ API Access: Full API access for custom integrations
- ✅ Webhooks: Real-time notifications and data sync
- ✅ Custom Branding: White-label options for your brand

**Integrations:** All Starter integrations plus Clover POS, advanced POS systems, Kitchen Display Systems, supply chain management, business intelligence tools, and custom integrations.

**Support:** Priority email (4-8 hour response), extended live chat, phone support during business hours, dedicated account manager, and onboarding assistance.
